My Resource Recommendations

For Genki I & II based learners

Genki Companion App

All the textbook vocabulary pre-organised into a flashcard and review app.

ToKini Andy (YouTube)

Structured video walkthroughs explaining grammar chapter-by-chapter, complete with nuance tips not found in the textbooks.

Good Exposure Material

Perfect for dedicated listening practice or active background immersion while doing chores. Nihongo con Teppei  is highly recommended for testing your comprehension against native content. While he may cover topics outside your current vocabulary, he speaks at an accessible pace, emphasising new words and using Katakana English or simpler Japanese to explain context.

For starting to step outside the textbook

Anki

The gold standard flashcard app, with SRS to leverage how your memory works. Build your own flashcard deck, or use/edit a pre-made community deck.

Kaishi 1.5k Deck

The premier vocabulary starter deck recommended for beginner-to-intermediate learners starting their anki and self-learning journey.

WaniKani

A structured, web-based alternative to Anki focusing purely on mnemonics to make mastering Kanji and associated vocabulary painless.

Natively

A community-graded book and manga database. Perfect for finding accessible reading practice tailored precisely to your current comprehension level.

For those travelling solo

If you are approaching Japanese completely on your own, know that there is no single "correct" path—only the path that keeps you consistently showing up every day.

Traditional routes usually centre around textbook curricula like Genki or Minna no Nihongo, which offer structured stepping stones. If you prefer a clear framework but want to study at your own pace, pairing textbooks with interactive companion apps is a great way to stay engaged.

Alternatively, modern self-study communities lean heavily into comprehensible input and early immersion, replacing traditional textbook drills with immediate exposure to books and native materials. If this approach excites you, head over to The Moe Way to explore a step-by-step roadmap dedicated to this methodology.
